About the Unit

A small team with an outsized mandate.

The Ozone Unit sits within the Department of Energy and Climate Change Affairs at MECENR. We license and monitor the import of ozone-depleting substances and HFCs, support refrigeration and air-conditioning (RAC) technicians with training and certification pathways, and represent Seychelles at regional and international ozone forums.

Licensing

Import/export permits for controlled substances.

RAC Support

Technician certification and good-practice guidance.

Compliance

National reporting to the Montreal Protocol Secretariat.

Montreal Protocol & Legislation

From CFCs to the Kigali Amendment.

Seychelles' ozone journey, and where our obligations stand today.

1992
Seychelles ratifies the Montreal Protocol
Formal commitment to phase out ozone-depleting substances.
2010
CFCs fully phased out
National import of chlorofluorocarbons brought to zero.
2019
Kigali Amendment ratified
Commitment to a phased reduction of HFCs, the current generation of refrigerants.
2024–2045
HFC phase-down schedule
Staged reduction targets for importers, aligned with the Kigali baseline.
